Another Original Framework
The Opportunity Equation
Opportunity = Need × Timing × Capability × Awareness × Action

If any factor is zero, the opportunity cannot be realized:

No need → no opportunity.

Wrong timing → opportunity stalls.

No capability → opportunity can't be executed.

No awareness → opportunity remains unseen.

No action → opportunity is lost.

You could then extend the model with additional multipliers such as trust, networks, resources, and persistence.

A Concept I Think Would Make the Book Stand Out
Many books focus only on "finding opportunities." I think you should present a broader taxonomy that shows opportunities can emerge in multiple ways:

Discovered Opportunities – Already exist but are unnoticed.

Created Opportunities – Brought into existence through innovation or entrepreneurship.

Invited Opportunities – Earned through reputation, relationships, and credibility.

Inherited Opportunities – Received through family, institutions, or prior investments.

Engineered Opportunities – Deliberately designed by building platforms, ecosystems, or markets.

Compounded Opportunities – New opportunities generated from successfully executing earlier ones.

Shared Opportunities – Created through partnerships and collaboration.

Transformational Opportunities – Arise from major technological, economic, or societal shifts.

This perspective reinforces the idea that opportunity is not merely something to be found—it can be cultivated, expanded, and multiplied.

About the Author and the Opportunity Ecosystem
My name is Bernard Baah, and I am the Founder and CEO of Filly Coder, a global software development and artificial intelligence company dedicated to building technologies that help individuals, businesses, and communities become smarter, more productive, and better connected.

Throughout my career, I have been fascinated by one question:

Why do some people consistently create success while others, with similar talents and resources, struggle to move forward?

After years of building businesses, developing software, mentoring entrepreneurs, and working with people from different backgrounds and countries, I came to believe that the answer is not simply intelligence, hard work, or even luck. At the heart of success lies opportunity—the ability to recognize it, create it, and act on it.

That belief inspired this book.

I believe that opportunity is one of the most powerful forces shaping our lives. Every career, business, invention, investment, relationship, and breakthrough begins with an opportunity. Yet opportunities are often invisible to those who are not prepared to see them. More importantly, many of the greatest opportunities are not found—they are deliberately created.

This philosophy has influenced every product and platform my company develops.

Rather than building software for technology's sake, our mission is to build systems that help people learn, work, collaborate, solve problems, and create meaningful progress.

Our growing ecosystem includes:

Coonected – An AI-powered Progress Network designed to help people uncover, create, share, and capture opportunities. Unlike traditional social networks that primarily optimize for engagement, Coonected is designed to help people make measurable progress in their careers, businesses, education, finances, relationships, and personal development. Throughout this book, Coonected will serve as a practical example of how technology can become an opportunity engine rather than simply a communication platform.

Filly Tutor – A global tutoring marketplace connecting students with qualified tutors while expanding educational opportunities for learners everywhere.

Filly Jobs – A platform that connects job seekers, freelancers, employers, and service professionals, making employment and income-generating opportunities more accessible.

Filly HR – Human resource management software designed to help organizations recruit, develop, and retain talent more effectively.

Hotesoft – A hotel management platform that helps hospitality businesses improve operational efficiency and customer experience.

Although this book explores opportunity from many perspectives—including psychology, business, leadership, economics, technology, education, and entrepreneurship—the underlying principle remains the same:

Technology should expand human potential, not replace it. Artificial intelligence should democratize opportunity, not concentrate it. The greatest innovations are those that empower more people to learn, build, collaborate, and create lasting value.
