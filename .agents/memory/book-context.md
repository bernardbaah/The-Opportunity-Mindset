## Book
**Title:** The Opportunity Mindset: How to Recognize, Create, and Capture Opportunity
**Author:** Bernard Baah, Founder & CEO of Filly Coder

## Structure
31 chapters across 8 parts:
- Part I: Developing the Opportunity Mindset (Ch 1–4)
- Part II: Recognizing Opportunity (Ch 5–8)
- Part III: Creating Opportunity (Ch 9–12)
- Part IV: Evaluating Opportunity (Ch 13–15)
- Part V: Capturing Opportunity (Ch 16–18)
- Part VI: Multiplying Opportunity (Ch 19–22)
- Part VII: Opportunity in Every Area of Life (Ch 23–28)
- Part VIII: The Future of Opportunity (Ch 29–31)

## Signature Frameworks
- The Opportunity Lifecycle: Recognize → Evaluate → Create → Capture → Expand → Multiply → Share
- The Opportunity Equation: Opportunity = Need × Timing × Capability × Awareness × Action
- The Opportunity Pyramid: survival → transformational opportunities
- The Opportunity Compass: Purpose, Potential, Preparedness, Payoff
- The Opportunity Flywheel: execution builds skills/trust/networks → greater opportunities

## Coonected Integration Strategy
- Coonected = AI-powered Progress Network (not a social network)
- Use a recurring **"In Practice: Coonected" sidebar** at the end of relevant chapters
- **Strong fits:** Ch 10 (Engineering Opportunity), Ch 11 (Relationships/networks), Ch 20 (Opportunity Systems), Ch 29 (AI), Ch 30 (Opportunity at Scale)
- **Avoid in:** psychology chapters (1–4), risk/evaluation chapters (13–15) — use third-party examples there
- Key rule: frameworks are the star; Coonected is the illustration, never the pitch
- Coonected covers: opportunity discovery, AI-assisted matching, networking, mentorship, collaboration, skill development, entrepreneurship, job creation, fundraising, partnerships, opportunity ecosystems

## Author Platforms
Filly Coder ecosystem: Coonected, Filly Tutor, Filly Learning, Filly Jobs, Filly Edu, Filly HR, Hotesoft
